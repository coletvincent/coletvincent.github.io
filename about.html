(More) About Me

I am originally from a suburb just outside of Charlotte, North Carolina where I grew up for the entirety of my childhood. After high school I moved to Raleigh to attend NC State University. I really only lived in Raleigh and spent time on campus during my sophomore and senior years at State since my first year was moved online due to covid and I spent my junior year abroad. In addition to my statistics studies, I took Japanese language courses throughout my first two years at NC State and also took them for a year at Nagoya Univeristy, where I spent my year abroad. This was my first time out of the country and it was filled with stress, loneliness, happiness, and excitement. I truly think I changed the most I have ever noticed in the year and learned to navigate life just a little bit better than I had done before. When I came back to the states for my final year, I knew I wanted to continue my education and thought about how I might be able to do that in Japan. Long story short, I ended up going throuhg the process of graduate school applications in Japan and found the program I am currently in now. Like anywhere, Japan has it's ups and downs like any other country, but I feel like the pace of life and the atmosphere in general is quite calming; I like it here. Perhaps my love might change, but for now, I am intent on polishing my Japanese and making the most of my education here.

Academic Interests

I know I said Bayesian Optimization in my intial introduction, but that is quite a broad topic, right? There are many things happening in this field right now and quite a lot of catching up for me to do. I think this subspace (ha) is a nice fit due to my statistical background and eagerness to learn more in terms of theoretical and application statistics. I still tend to find myself struggling quite a bit to fully understand concepts sometimes, but I think it would be more concerning if I wasn't feeling this way. I am particularly interested in high-dimensional combonatorial spaces in BO. There's a lot to be explored here, and I am eager to find my place.

In addition, I have an interest in applying BO to a wide range of AI tasks and problems. I am still trying to narrow down my interests exactly, but I imagine I'll lean in a direction of generative artificial intelligence as it relates to game and content generation. There are some ideas I have within music production that I would like to experiment with, but I feel like I lack some experience here.

Hobbies, etc.

Background and Research Interests

I have a B.S. in Statistics from North Carolina State University in Raleigh, North Carolina.

I am currently in my final semester of my Master's program at Tohoku University. As part of my graduation requirements, I will submit a research thesis outlining the research and results I conducted during my program. My thesis is focused on the integration of the induced geometry of generative models into the training loop and understanding the effects it has. I will post more information, and potentially the manuscript, when available.

I initially started my journey in research with a focus on Bayesian optimization, looking into Gaussian Processes and understanding how they can be compared to multilayer perceptrons when used in a neural network. With a few changes, and some papers read, my interest in Differential Geometry and the applications to generative modeling grew. This topic, although somewhat foreign, aligns better to the focuses of our lab and the expertise that surrounds me.

My research interests are in machine learning applications of mathematical and statistical methods. Generally speaking, my research will broadly fall into the one or more of the following topics:

  • Generative Modelling
  • Deep Learning
  • Information / Differential Geometry
  • Bayesian Optimization



I am currently honing my skills and intuition to better my ability to conduct meaningful research. If you have any interest, comments, or just want to reach out, please feel free to do so through my contact page. I will post more details about published materials and presentations later on below this.

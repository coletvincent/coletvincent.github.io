Background and Research Interests

I have a B.S. in Statistics from North Carolina State University in Raleigh, North Carolina.
I also hold a Master's of Information Sciences from Tohoku University in Sendai, Japan.

I am currently in my final semester of my Master's program at Tohoku University. As part of my graduation requirements, I will submit a research thesis outlining the research and results I conducted during my program. My thesis is focused on the integration of the induced geometry of generative models into the training loop and understanding the effects it has. I will post more information, and potentially the manuscript, when available.

I initially started my journey in research with a focus on Bayesian optimization, looking into Gaussian Processes and understanding how they can be compared to multilayer perceptrons when used in a neural network. With a few changes, and some papers read, my interest in Differential Geometry and the applications to generative modeling grew. This topic, although somewhat foreign, aligns better to the focuses of our lab and the expertise that surrounds me.

My research interests are in machine learning applications of mathematical and statistical methods. Generally speaking, my research will broadly fall into the one or more of the following topics:

  • Generative Modelling
  • Deep Learning
  • Information / Differential Geometry
  • Bayesian Optimization

My skills cover a pretty broad scope of topics, but follow along the path in which I took to get to where I am now.

For coding languages, I am most familiar with Python and R, though I mainly use Python on a day to day basis and would call it my strongest language. I have a strong working knowledge of PyTorch, which I prefer to work with over Tensorflow or Jax. I am familiar with common data cleaning techniques in both R (tinyverse) and Python (using pandas, polars, etc.), which are also backed by some of the classes I took while an undergraduate. I know some SAS, though I have not used it in a very long time. I self-taught myself HTML and some CSS, though I wouldn't necessarily put this on a resume.

For theoretical mathematics, I have taken an extensive amount of classes, but these are skewed towards statistical theory rather than pure mathematics. Some classes I would highlight are two semesters of Statistical Mathematics at NCSU, more statistical mathematics and graph theory at Tohoku U, group theory at Nagoya U, and a mixture of machine learning and computational mathematics / statistics at all of these institutions. After everything, I found myself enjoying machine learning from the perspective of topology and grew an interest in Differential Geometry, Riemannian Geometry, and Gaussian Processes.

If you have any interest, comments, or just want to reach out, please feel free to do so through my contact page.

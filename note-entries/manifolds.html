A Brief Introduction to Manifolds

What is a manifold?

A manifold can be thought of as a shape of any dimension, though you'll only ever see them talked about in the 3D sense. These shapes have to adhere by specific rules, and can also inherit special properties that make them very, very useful in certain areas. We will talk about what makes some of these properties desirable later on. For now, we can rigorously define a manifold as an \(n\text{-dimensional}\) structure that is locally Euclidean.
